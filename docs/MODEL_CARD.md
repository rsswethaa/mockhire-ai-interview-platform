# Model Card — Rule Baseline v1

## Intended use
Educational demonstration of explainable interview-response scoring.

## Inputs
Answer text and predefined competency keywords.

## Outputs
Relevance, evidence, clarity and weighted overall score.

## Method
Transparent lexical coverage, action/result evidence signals and answer-length heuristic.

## Known limitations
The method does not understand full semantic context, culture, disability-related communication differences, multilingual nuance or job validity. Scores are not calibrated to hiring outcomes.

## Prohibited use
Autonomous rejection, ranking without human review, protected-trait inference or high-stakes employment decisions.
