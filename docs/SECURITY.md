# Security, Privacy and Hiring Safety

Interview answers and applicant identity are sensitive employment data.

Production controls should include authentication, least-privilege RBAC, TLS, encryption at rest, secrets management, audit logs, retention/deletion controls and breach response procedures.

## Decision safety
Scores must remain decision-support only. Do not infer protected characteristics, emotion, personality, honesty or suitability from biometric/appearance proxies. Any real hiring workflow needs human review, bias testing, accessible alternatives and job-related validation.
